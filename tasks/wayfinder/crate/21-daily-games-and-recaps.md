## Question

Which daily music games (Heardle and its successors, Bandle, Spotle and the like) and automated recap formats (Wrapped-style stories, shareable round summaries, league newsletters) exist in late 2026; how do they source audio legally, how do they grow and make money, and which of them died and why?

_Context:_ Jordi's raw ideas (ticket 12) add a daily game and per-league stories and newsletters. This ticket states the as-is market for those formats before the grilling; it does not decide whether Crate has them.

## Resolution

Resolved 2026-11-22 by crate-session-2. Findings: [research/daily-games-and-recaps.md](research/daily-games-and-recaps.md). Checked against its brief by the main session.

**Answer, in brief:**
- **No daily music game documents a public licence for the commercial audio it plays.** Bandle recreates each song itself and says licensing is costly; others use SoundCloud embeds, Apple preview URLs or self-hosted clips.
- **No-audio formats get traction:** Spotle (artist from attribute tiles), Harmonies, Lyricle (lyrics licensing not researched).
- **Heardle:** bought by Spotify in July 2022 and shut on 5 May 2023 by its owner, not by a takedown; visits had fallen from 69M (March 2022) to 6M (March 2023).
- **Money is small:** song packs, ads, subscriptions, ad-free fees.
- **Growth is share-your-result plus creators** (Wordle grid; Bandle via streamers).
- **Recaps:** Wrapped 2025 had 575M shares (reported); an unaffiliated "Music League Wrapped" exists; no automated league newsletter found. Cut Club, YapZap and TuneBoxed already bolt a daily mode onto leagues.
- **Free web sharing:** Web Share API with files, Open Graph cards, text grids, PNG download; Instagram Stories and TikTok sharing are native-only.

**Still open:** Rights behind lessgames' clips; how Bandle licenses; whether lyrics games license lyrics; Variety's Wrapped figures.
