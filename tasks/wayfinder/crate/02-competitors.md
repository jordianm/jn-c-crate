## Question

Who competes with Music League today, and how does each one compare on mechanics, music services (and how each is integrated), platforms, price and business model, traction and liveness?

_Context:_ Tells which differentiators are taken and which are open.

## Resolution

Resolved 2026-11-22 by crate-overnight #1 (research) and crate-session-2 (closing review). Findings: [research/competitors.md](research/competitors.md). Checked against its brief by the main session.

**Answer, in brief:**
- **No league clone has traction:** BandJam leads with 16 iOS ratings and "1K+" Play downloads; YapZap, Cut Club and DJ League are smaller. Music League has 6,586 iOS ratings and "100K+".
- **The only big numbers are live, short-session web games:** songbattle.io (349,645 games, 28.8M votes) and Aux Battles (14,360 players), both free.
- **Service breadth, guess-who, chat, reminders and analytics are already shipped** by one rival or another (YapZap lists 7 services; BandJam has guess-who, chat, nudges, 23 charts). Study 01's gaps 3-6 are occupied.
- **Models:** BandJam and Aux Battles free with no ads; DJ League ads plus about $4/mo (from code); Cut Club charges the host ($9.99 a season or $99.99 a year).
- **Nothing Spanish-language found;** several rivals localize their UI, so language alone is thin.
- **Open lane (inference):** distribution, not features; nobody pairs an async league with a live mode.

**Still open:** YapZap pricing and integration; real active users of any clone; whether any clone has Spotify extended quota.
