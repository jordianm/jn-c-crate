## Question

What do players of Music League and its alternatives value, complain about and ask for? Cluster the themes, with quotes, links and the strength of each.

_Context:_ Separates evidenced gaps from guessed ones.

## Resolution

Resolved 2026-11-22 by crate-session-2. Findings: [research/player-sentiment.md](research/player-sentiment.md). Checked against its brief by the main session.

**Answer, in brief:**
- **Sample:** 458 written App Store reviews of Music League, 9 storefronts, 2022-02 to 2026-11. Written reviews skew negative (42% 1★) against a 4.65 store rating. Reddit could not be reached.
- **Ads on mobile dominate:** 62% of reviews, 86% in 2026; they gate results and standings, and reviewers say leagues die of the friction.
- **Reliability and sign-in failures** (about 95 reviews) turn into missed deadlines.
- **Spotify lock-in** is the steadiest feature gap (about 30 reviews, 2022-2026); YouTube is still missing in 2.0.
- **What players love is social:** friends first, discovery second, competition third.
- **Guess-who-submitted** is a small but unprompted ask (6); rules complaints are mostly answered by 2.0; accessibility is 3 reviews with severe effect.
- **Nobody asks for a daily or live game** (absence in a sample of current users, not proof of no demand).

**Still open:** Reddit r/musicleague (blocked to automated access: needs Jordi to export threads by hand); how the 2.0 beta lands; whether a second service brings new players or only keeps current ones.
