## Question

Which engagement and virality mechanics are documented to work in consumer apps with a social or habit loop (Duolingo streaks, leagues and leaderboards, BeReal, Wordle's share grid, Strava clubs and kudos, Spotify Blend and Wrapped, Locket, Gas, Geoguessr duels), what evidence exists for each (company reports, published experiments, retention figures), and which costs or harms are documented (streak anxiety, dark-pattern rules, the EU DSA and consumer-law limits on manipulative design)?

_Context:_ Jordi's second batch of raw ideas (ticket 12) asks how Duolingo-style mechanics and shareable content could make a friends' music league viral and social. This states the as-is evidence; it does not choose mechanics.
