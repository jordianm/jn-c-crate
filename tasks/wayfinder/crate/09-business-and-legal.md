## Question

Which business models fit the music platforms' rules and this market, and which EU and Spain legal basics (GDPR, digital-consent age, cookies, LSSI, DSA, consumer law, linking and embedding, running it as an individual) apply to a small social music game run by one person?

_Context:_ Feeds the business-model and ambition decisions. Not legal advice.

## Resolution

Resolved 2026-11-22 by crate-session-2. Findings: [research/business-and-legal.md](research/business-and-legal.md). Checked against its brief by the main session.

**Answer, in brief:**
- **Links to official pages and the platforms' own embeds are lawful in EU copyright law** (Svensson, BestWater, VG Bild-Kunst); the exposure is contract terms, chiefly Spotify's Widget Terms on games.
- **A product that only links out accepts no developer agreement** (inference); embeds bring in Spotify Widget Terms and YouTube API terms; MusicKit brings in Apple's monetisation ban.
- **Audio clips: host none by default.** Ranked: link-out; the platform's own embed started by the listener; raw preview files (breach of Spotify, Apple, Deezer terms); a licensed clip provider such as 7digital (paid, price unpublished); self-hosted clips (three sets of rights; Spanish quotation law does not cover a game).
- **No free, lawful blind clip exists** (inference): free embeds show title and artwork.
- **Business models, safest first:** free; donations; host pays for the game's own features; affiliate links (Amazon.es pays 3 EUR per Music Unlimited trial); sponsorship; display ads.
- **Law from the first user:** GDPR controller duties; consent valid from 14 in Spain; newsletters need prior consent (LSSI art. 21); cookies need consent (art. 22.2), so load embeds on click; DSA hosting duties with the micro/small exemption.
- **Running it as an individual:** no safe income threshold for registering as self-employed (STS 941/2025; SMI 17,094 EUR in 2026).

**Still open:** Whether a pasted-link league with Spotify embeds is "game functionality"; cover art and metadata in generated stories; minors' terms; paid-feature consumer wording. Needs a lawyer before launch with money.
