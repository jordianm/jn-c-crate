## Question

What exactly does Music League offer today, in the rebuilt version (next.musicleague.com) and in the legacy apps (mechanics, settings, limits, reminders, social features, music services, plans and prices, relaunch timeline), and how does it square with Spotify's "Do not create a game" rule?

_Context:_ It is the product to beat. Resolves open questions 1-5 of study 01.

## Resolution

Resolved 2026-11-22 by crate-overnight #1. Findings: [research/music-league-today.md](research/music-league-today.md) (pages opened directly; the main session re-checked three claims on the live pages: the "Beta version of 2.0" wording and its 2026-11-12 date, the 16-year minimum age in the Terms, and the reminder channels; all three matched).

**Answer, in brief:**
- Music League 2.0 is a **public beta** since at least 2026-11-12; the store apps are still the legacy 1.x app and the new iPhone app is TestFlight only.
- 2.0 already has **league chat, song threads, @mentions, follow/block, an activity feed, public leagues, a global Charts page and a Year-in-Review**. Direct messages are "not available yet".
- **Reminders:** in-app, email and push, on by default, configurable per type; about 24 h before each deadline to players who have not acted.
- **Music services:** Spotify and Apple Music only (search, playback, playlists in each player's own library). No YouTube Music.
- **Money:** free with ads (Nitro on the web); Premium = ad removal + CSV export, US $2.99/month or $14.99/year. No higher tier exists.
- **Spotify's game ban:** unexplained. The only primary statement is the CEO in 2025: Spotify is "kind enough to allow it".

**Supersedes in study 01:** "no public leagues yet" and "no evidence of player-to-player chat" (both wrong for 2.0); the "$24.99 tier" (regional prices); the four-CSV export (legacy only; 2.0 exports one CSV, Premium only). Full list in the findings' "Corrections to study 01".

**Still open:** tie-breaking (undocumented), 2.0 Premium price (login-gated), how Music League squares with Spotify's policy (needs Spotify or a lawyer).
