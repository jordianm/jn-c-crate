## Question

What free, terms-compatible sources can a small commercial-leaning music game use to (a) detect trending songs (Apple Music RSS / marketing feeds, YouTube charts, Spotify Charts, Shazam, Billboard, Last.fm, ListenBrainz, Kworb-like trackers) and (b) suggest songs to a player from the songs they upvoted (Last.fm similar tracks, ListenBrainz recommendations, MusicBrainz relations, own co-vote data across leagues), with limits, licences and commercial-use terms for each; and what do existing products do for discovery (Spotify Discover Weekly and Blend, Music League, the clones)?

_Context:_ Jordi's second batch (ticket 12): a weekly playlist of suggested songs as links, built from a player's upvotes, and trending songs brought into the game. Constraint: links only, free tiers, legally safe base. Builds on song-matching and other-services findings.
